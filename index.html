Questions customers may ask.

Can I print invoices?

Yes. Use the Print / Save PDF button in the app to print or save the invoice as a PDF.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Logo upload is available exclusively on Pro Unlimited and Business Unlimited plans.

Can I use Valora EM offline?

Yes. Offline mode is included with the Business Unlimited plan. Invoices created offline are saved locally on the same device and browser; cloud sync after reconnect is planned for the next production update.

How will payments work?

You can securely pay using credit cards, debit cards, GCash, Maya, QR Ph supported banking/e-wallet apps, and supported online banking options once checkout is active. Available methods depend on the payment provider.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade your subscription plan anytime through your account to unlock more features.

Is there a refund policy?

No. All subscription payments are final and non-refundable once account access is activated, except where required by law or payment provider rules.
