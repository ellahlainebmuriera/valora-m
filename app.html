    Learning Center

    Simple guided steps for setting up Valora EM, creating documents, and tracking your business.

    New user guide

    Start here if this is your first time.

    Follow the steps below from business setup to printing your first invoice. You can always come back to this tab when you need help.

    1

    Set up your business profile

    Add your store name, contact details, currency, receipt colors, print style, and logo if your plan supports it.

    2

    Add customers

    Create a client list so invoices and estimates can be filled faster with saved customer details.

    3

    Create an invoice or estimate

    Choose a document type, add line items, discount, tax, shipping, notes, photos, and optional signatures.

    4

    Preview and print

    Switch between PDF/A4 and thermal receipt styles, then use Print / Save PDF when the document looks right.

    5

    Track payments and history

    Use Invoice History to review saved documents, filter by date or status, and update records when customers pay.

    6

    Monitor profit

    Add expenses so the dashboard can show net profit instead of only total sales.

    Offline Mode

    Create invoices and print PDFs even when internet is unstable.

    How offline access works
    Local browser storage

    Invoices, clients, business settings, logo, colors, and signature are saved in this browser so you can keep working on the same device.

    PDF or thermal printing

    Choose PDF / A4, Thermal 80mm, or Thermal 58mm in Settings before using Print / Save PDF.

    Offline login rule

    Open the app online once first. After it is cached, existing accounts can keep working offline on the same browser and device. New devices still need internet first.
