Subscription Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Digital Nature of Service:

Valora EM provides a digital Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform. Because premium features and layout access tokens are granted instantly upon verification, all subscription sales (both Monthly and Yearly plans) are strictly final and non-refundable.

2. Cancellation:

You can cancel your subscription at any time via Account Settings. Upon cancellation, paid tier access persists until the expiration of the current billing cycle. No subsequent renewals will charge.

3. Billing System Errors:

Valora EM operates as a fully automated platform. In the rare event of a system glitch where a user is double-charged or a paid plan fails to trigger automatically, please submit a report directly through the In-App Bug Reporting System within forty-eight (48) hours of the transaction for manual validation and credit adjustments.
