Questions customers may ask.

Can I print invoices?

Yes. Use the Print / Save PDF button in the app to print or save the invoice as a PDF.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Paid plans include store logo upload and custom invoice colors.

Does it work without Supabase?

Yes. Customers can create an account, generate invoices, save client records, and print or save PDFs from the web app.

How will payments work?

Payment links can be connected to PayMongo, Stripe, Gumroad, or manual GCash while starting out.
